What is PerfectAmino Coated Tabs?

PerfectAmino comes in three forms. The uncoated tablets (the original). The coated tablets (BHPACT6001, the one you might be looking at). And a flavored powder. The active formula is the same in all three — eight essential amino acids in a specific ratio. The coated tablets have a thin vegetable-based film over each one that makes them slide down easier and tastes less chalky. The bottle is huge (600 tablets), but the serving is five tablets, and the label suggests taking one to three servings a day. So that's anywhere from 40 days down to 13 days of supply depending on how much you're taking. The 'free-form' part means the amino acids aren't bound up in actual protein — your body absorbs them directly without having to digest anything first. That's why people use them pre-workout (faster than waiting for a meal to digest) and why older adults sometimes do well on them (your digestion of intact protein gets less efficient with age).
